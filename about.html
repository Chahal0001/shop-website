About Us

Our story, since 2018.

From a small workshop in Trikuta Nagar to one of the region's largest tyre & automobile suppliers — here's how Chahal Engineers & Co. came to be.

We started our business in 2018 with a vision to serve the erstwhile state of Jammu & Kashmir with the best quality tyres and wheels. At the time, the region had limited availability of internationally renowned brands, and we saw an opportunity to introduce completely new products and provide customers with better choices and quality.

What began as a small workshop has grown into one of the largest suppliers in the state. It has been an incredible journey of learning, growth, and perseverance, and our journey continues.
